Abstract
This thesis project describes the development of a mobile microlearning application
that helps adult learners prepare for the International English Language Testing System
(IELTS) through games. Previous reviews of language learning and IELTS applications
have pointed to a clear lack of tools that could effectively combine gamification, mi
crolearning, and adult-oriented design. This thesis discusses a mobile application that
will consist of five educational games. Each of them addressing specific and more than
one basic IELTS-related language skill.

1.1 Problem Background
The International English Language Testing System (IELTS) is a well-known interna
tional examination. It is owned by University of Cambridge ESOL Examination and
the British Council, IDP IELTS, which is used to assess the level of English language
proficiency of people who wish to pursue a career or studies in an English speaking envi
ronment [1].

Interestingly, the IELTS tests that occur every year around the world have been in
creasing (See Figure 1.1). You can see that the tests that are provided each year have
increased by about four times over the last sixteen years. This continuous growth marks
the long existing global plea for the IELTS test as a proper method of assessing English
language proficiency [2], [3], [4]. This demand is supported by the level of acceptance

knowledge of English is also anticipated in an English-speaking job even when English is

2.1 Language Apps
Duolingo is the widely known language-learning app that the world recognizes, meant
to make the process of learning new languages easy and fun. With more than 30 mil
lion users, who can freely download the app to their mobile phones, it becomes very
accessible for all learners [10], [11], [12]. There is empirical evidence of the effectiveness
of active skills taught by Duolingo, especially in vocabulary acquisition. For instance,
in the study of Zholdas et al. (2024), it was found that adult learners of languages
who were using Duolingo acquired vocabulary and grammatical skills. However, they did
not gain the competence for practical use of the language to use it in communication [10].
In a study by Kessler et al. (2023) is Babbel rated higher by users than Duolingo for
grammar, speaking and pronunciation practice, and exposure to the target culture [13].
This is a free-formed application for learners inclined toward more open discovery of the
language [14].
Also, Memrise is another common app that mixes fun features with useful learning
tools. One study that used Memrise as a tool for learning French is the one by Paradhina
et al. (2021), which actually successfully elicited positive results. However, it is imper
ative to note that this study pertained to the French language and not to the English
language [15].
Busuu offers short lessons and little bits of it, which supports individual learning of a
language [16]. According to a study by K´etyi (2016) learners using Busuu scored much
higher than those from the controlled group, where the app was not used [17]. Busuu
shows the value of game-like features in making users more interested and helping learn
ing [16].
HelloTalk is a mobile application available on both iOS and Android platforms, de
signed to practice interactive language. According to a study by Taufan et al. (2022),
HelloTalk supports the developmental process of languages as it connects a person to a
native speaker so that the proficiency can be enhanced through real-time communications
[18].

3.1 Our product
To identify our product as a solution, we used the 5 W method:
• What– What are you offering?
A mobile Android-based microlearning game for IELTS exam preparation. Since
there are several perceived benefits of mobile microlearning tools for adult learners
in the context of professional development [28].
• Who– Who uses your product?
The people are between 15 to 50 years old and are preparing for the IELTS exam.
This is especially for the people who want flexible and interesting learning materials.
• Where– Where is it will be offered?
Will be available on the Android platform (Google Play) at any time and any place.
Since Android smartphones are more used than iPhones in developing countries [29].
• When– When do users need this app?
When readying themselves for the IELTS test, getting rid of the issues tied to time
limits and ineffective study methods.
• Why– Why should users choose your app?
For its convenient, engaging, and bite-sized IELTS preparation designed for adult
learners, especially when time and access to formal training are at a premium.

3.3 Implementation
3.3.1 Design
The design of the project was the first thing that we began to consider after producing
the initial concept for the game we intended to create. The frameworks we selected are
modular design framework and player-centric minimalism.
The modular design framework was chosen because it requires every element (but
tons, blank spaces, text etc.) to be placed in a separate block. This approach structures
the design like a puzzle, where every block has its own place. One more benefit of this
frameworks is that changes do not affect the overall layout and any necessary corrections
can be made on individual blocks without disruption of any other components.
Applying player-centric minimalism allowed to maintain focus on educational purpose
of the game. Every element of the application serves a specific purpose, there are no dis
tracting elements that could shift the players attention from learning. Moreover, this
approach makes it easier for players to engage with the content and navigate the game
smoothly.
Color analysis
When it came to the color choice, we wanted the color scheme to be calming and non
intrusive to keep our users focused, which is especially important for an educational game.
In the end, we decided to go with the purple color gradients. This would also add modern
touch to the game. Besides, due to the high contrast of the chosen colors, the interface
has high readability. The white and light-colored text/buttons stand out against the
darker background, focusing the attention of the viewer in one spot.
It is important to also address the psychology behind the color scheme, purples are
often associated with imagination, intelligence, and achievement. Since the game is de
signed to help users learn and improve their language skills, this color naturally supports
the idea and the values that we put behind this game (See Figure 3.1).

Framework
For the backend part of our application, we used a tool from Google called Firebase. Fire
base provides a convenient set of services that facilitate and speed up the development
and scaling of mobile applications. Firebase was chosen because of its backend infras

[34]. And all of this allows us to avoid developing any server-side code.
We used tools such as:
• Kotlin Coroutines: allows the application to remain responsive during time-consuming

• Dagger/Hilt: automatically sets dependencies, making code more convenient and
saving development time [38].
• StateFlow/MutableStateFlow: needed to store and transfer data so that the inter
face displays it immediately [39].
These tools make Firebase effective for building the backend of mobile applications,
ensuring performance and extensibility.

3.3.3 Front-End
Front-end development is a crucial aspect of building modern user interfaces and is re
sponsible for the part of the application that the end user directly interacts with. It
consists of visual elements such as buttons, input fields, pages, and also the interaction
behavior of these elements. The front-end strives to provide a clear, intuitive, and aes
thetically pleasing interface that meets the expectations and needs of the use.
On a student exchange basis, for the purpose of developing a mobile application, the
front-end is developed keeping in mind responsive design, interaction with the back-end,
and demands of modern user experience (UX) [44]. All the UI parts of the app are
coded under a component-based structure and with code-reusability focused methodolo
gies, keeping in mind the extensibility of the project on a grand scale.
To implement the interface, the Jetpack Compose library is utilized, which provides a
declarative way of specifying the UI in Android applications [45]. It allows the developer
to specify the interface as a function of the current state of the data, simplifying the
display logic and making the code more readable. The visual look is established using
Material Design components, which give a consistent and modern interface appearance
[46].
Thus, the front-end of the application not only affects the users’ visual experience of
the platform, but it also plays an important role in affecting the usability, interaction
speed and overall effectiveness of the digital product.
Purpose and Tasks of the Front-End
The main goal of the front-end part of the mobile application is to create a simple and
intuitive interface that offers smooth navigation and easy access to the functionality of
the platform. Users should be able to register, see practice materials, take mock tests,
track their progress, and update their profile without needing additional instructions.
The key tasks of the front-end within the framework of this project include:
• Implementation of a multi-screen interface with seamless navigation between core
sections (registration, login, practice tests, learning materials, progress tracking,
user profile, etc.).
• Handling user input and displaying appropriate feedback such as error messages or
confirmation notices (e.g., during test submission or form completion).
• Integration with the backend and Firebase to fetch and display up-to-date content,
scores, and learning progress in real time.
• Ensuring responsive design to support various screen sizes and resolutions.
• Applying a modern visual style and animations to enhance user experience and
engagement.
22
• Improving accessibility through features like dark mode support, scalable fonts, and
clear UI components for better readability and comfort during long study sessions.
The front-end is not just the "face" of the app but also a compelling aspect of the
business logic, enabling user behavior patterns and providing instant feedback to interac
tions. It is especially crucial in an IELTS practice app, where responsiveness, simplicity,
and reliability are necessary to maintain user interest and to offer a seamless learning
experience throughout each session.

Chapter 4
Results and Discussion
The insights gained from the literature review clearly indicated a gap in existing tools:
while many language apps offer gamified experiences and IELTS apps provide exam
oriented content, very few combine both in a user-centered, time-efficient format tailored
for adult learners. Our application attempted to address these gaps by introducing five
distinct games targeting core IELTS language skills, designed to be completed in short,
focused sessions.
To check if use and the results, we did a user test on a small group with five adult
learners aged between 15 and 50 years. All in all, the feedback proved right the main
ideas of the project. That is a game-like, small-bit- by-small-bit IELTS app could give
useful extra help for adult learners.
This application does not replace the traditional preparation for IELTS examinations
but it will be more successful as a supplementary tool that will help modern digital habits
and the unique needs among adult learners.

Chapter 5
Conclusion
This project aimed to meet the actual challenges of adult learners in IELTS preparation,
which are time management, psychological factors, and unavailability flexible learning
aids. Analysis of the existing apps and studies revealed a big gap in the market for the
type of mobile solution that offers gamified microlearning for adults.
We decided to create a microlearning mobile app. It has five interactive games that
are designed to enhance basic IELTS-related language skills. The system architecture for
our app ensures both scalability and responsiveness whereas the user interface has clarity
and ease of use.
In summary, the developed app successfully meets the goals of providing an easy
supplementary tool for adult IELTS prep, though it does not replace traditional methods
of study, it enhances them.
